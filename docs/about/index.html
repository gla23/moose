    About Gracious Moose

    Behind the designs

    Amaris Andersen is a self-taught freelance designer who loves bringing the pages of the Bible to life in art form. Her designs started out in her journalling Bible as a way to help her reflect on, learn and love God's word.

    Custom Designs and Commissions

    If you've got a favourite verse or quote that you'd love to see turned into a beautiful print you've come to the right place. Amaris loves creating unique designs that can express some of the beauty of the words most precious to you. If you're looking for something completely different to what you see here then we also might be able to help. Why not Get in Touch with the button to the right to start bringing your ideas to life.
